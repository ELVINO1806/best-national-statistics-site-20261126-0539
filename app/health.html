Health

Living longer, still catching up

A newborn in Indonesia can expect 71.3 years of life. Child deaths keep falling and stunting is down by nearly eight points since 2019 — but health spending is still under 3% of GDP, and there is only about one doctor for every 2,000 people.

Stunting is falling

Stunting — children growing too slowly for their age — fell from 27.7% in 2019 to 19.8% in 2024. It stalled in 2023, then dropped again. The government's earlier target was 14% by 2024, which was not reached.

Reading the stunting gap. The national rate is 19.8%, but the 2024 survey found 40.0% in Highland Papua and 37.0% in East Nusa Tenggara. A single national average hides the places where the problem is worst.
