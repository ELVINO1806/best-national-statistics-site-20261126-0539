Education

More years in school, slowly

The average Indonesian adult has now spent 8.85 years in school — up by almost a full year since 2015. Nine years of schooling are compulsory, and the state budget for education reached Rp 665 trillion in 2024.

A decade of schooling

Switch between the schooling adults actually received and the schooling a child starting today can expect. Both climb steadily, but the gap between them — about four years — shows how much schooling has expanded in a single generation.

How students perform

In the OECD's PISA 2022 assessment of 15-year-olds, Indonesia scored 366 in mathematics, 359 in reading and 383 in science — below the OECD averages of 472, 476 and 485, but up on the country's 2018 results in mathematics and reading.

PISA 2022 scores

PISA points, 15-year-olds. Source: OECD, PISA 2022.

The gap within the country. Mean schooling in Jakarta is 11.49 years; in Highland Papua it is 5.10 years. The national average of 8.85 years sits between two very different Indonesias.
