Population

281.6 million people

Indonesia is the world's fourth most populous country. Its population grew by about 2.9 million people in 2024, and nearly three in five Indonesians now live in towns and cities.

Why two population totals? BPS counts 281.6 million people in 2024; the United Nations counts 283.5 million for the same year. The gap comes from different methods — BPS builds on the 2020 census and its own projections, the UN on its global model. This site uses the BPS figure as its headline and names the publisher wherever the UN figure appears.
