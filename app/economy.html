Economy

A US$1.40 trillion economy

Indonesia is Southeast Asia's largest economy and the world's 16th largest by nominal GDP. Growth has held near 5% a year since the pandemic, while inflation fell to 1.57% in 2024 — its lowest in years.

Source: BPS (Statistics Indonesia), 2024. Unemployment and poverty both fell against the same months of 2023: unemployment was 5.32% in August 2023, and the poverty rate was 9.36% in September 2023.

What the numbers mean. At 5.03% growth with 1.57% inflation, 2024 was a steady year: output rose while prices barely moved. The poverty line is a spending line — someone counts as poor when their monthly spending per person falls below Rp 595,242, not when their income does.
